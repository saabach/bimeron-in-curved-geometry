# Repository scope and provenance

This repository presents the computational and formal component of research on
curved-surface ferromagnetism and bimeron-related analytical models.

## Included

- Original Lean declarations and proofs, project configuration and dependency pins.
- A calculation map connecting physical results, hypotheses and named Lean theorems.
- Standalone scientific explanations of conventions and limits.
- Original formula-generated figures and their reproduction script.
- A small consistency/axiom audit requiring no private report files.

The map preserves its original Portuguese scientific entries; navigation and the
archival introduction are adapted to the public structure. The 15 Lean files,
including the library importer and axiom inspection file, retain their original
mathematical content. No theorem was weakened, removed or modified to compile.

The old audit read private report chapters. Its public replacement checks the
map's retained equation identifiers, all theorem references and axiom-audit
coverage. It does not claim to audit any current external report.

## Excluded

Report and poster sources, templates, bibliographies used for document production,
all PDFs, typesetting/build artifacts, dependency caches, copied journal figures,
logos, third-party articles/books, private historical reviews and abandoned
experiments are outside this selection. Referenced `.tex` names in comments and
in the map are archival identifiers; those files are absent and unnecessary.

The public tree contains no copy of the document-generation chain. Python output
is restricted to scientific SVG/PNG illustrations and CSV samples. The research
originals remain separate and unchanged. No interface-growth project or dataset
is included.

## Research context

The research documentation identifies PIBIC/UFV, the 2025–2026 selection cycle,
Department of Physics, Universidade Federal de Viçosa, and supervision by
Jakson Miranda Fonseca. The work plan is dated June 2025 and describes a 12-month
activity schedule; precise scholarship start/end dates are not inferred from it.
The presentation acknowledges CNPq financial support. No grant identifier or
additional funding organization is inferred from logos in unrelated templates.

## Attribution, privacy and review

The retained code and selected figure generators originate in this project's
research materials. All distributed images are regenerated from those routines;
no third-party artwork is copied. References are bibliographic links only.
Dependencies are declared, not vendored; their own licenses apply to their
separately downloaded distributions.

No license is added here. Licensing and the decision to publish remain for the
researcher's review. Academic author/supervisor names and affiliation are retained
intentionally; personal contact details, local machine paths, credentials and
user account identifiers are excluded from the public files.
